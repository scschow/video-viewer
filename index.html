Keyboard shortcuts

Formats: MP4 / MOV / WebM as supported by your browser. H.264 plays everywhere; HEVC (iPhone “High Efficiency” capture) needs Safari or a hardware-decode-capable Chrome/Edge. On iPhone you can switch to “Most Compatible” in Settings → Camera → Formats.

On a phone: Share → Add to Home Screen runs FrameView fullscreen without browser bars.
